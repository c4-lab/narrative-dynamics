# Understanding Narrative Dynamics

## How Do Stories Shape Our Understanding of the World?

People naturally create stories to make sense of complex events and information. These narratives help us organize knowledge, connect disparate facts, and form coherent understandings of our experiences and surroundings. But how exactly do these stories form and spread within populations? What determines which narratives take hold and which fade away?

Our research investigates these fundamental questions by examining the interplay between narrative structure and social dynamics.

## Key Research Questions

- **How do people incorporate new information into existing narratives?** When confronted with new information, people must decide whether and how it fits into their current understanding.

- **Why do some stories spread rapidly while others struggle to gain traction?** Beyond social influence, we examine how the internal coherence and causal structure of narratives affect their transmission.

- **How do populations navigate competing or contradictory narrative elements?** When groups encounter information that challenges existing narratives, complex dynamics emerge between social and cognitive factors.

## Our Approach

Our project combines rigorous experimental methods with computational modeling to understand narrative diffusion. By designing controlled experiments where participants share narrative elements through social networks, we can observe how stories evolve and spread in real time.

We've developed mathematical models that quantify narrative influence based on causal connections between story elements. Our research shows that people are more likely to adopt new information when it connects meaningfully to their existing narrative framework.

{% include feature_row %}

## Beyond Right and Wrong

This research isn't about judging the accuracy of any particular narrative. Rather, we're interested in the underlying dynamics that govern how diverse populations construct and share stories. By understanding these processes, we gain insight into fundamental aspects of human communication and social cognition.

Our findings have implications for how communities process information, how knowledge spreads through populations, and how groups navigate shared understanding in an increasingly complex information landscape.
