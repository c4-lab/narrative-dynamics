# Research Datasets

Below you'll find datasets from our experimental studies on narrative dynamics. All datasets are provided under the MIT License and are available for research and educational purposes.

{% include coming-soon.html title="Experimental Datasets" message="We're currently preparing our experimental datasets for public release. ## Planned Datasets

### Network Experiment Data (2023-2024)

<div class="dataset-card">
  <div class="dataset-status">Coming Soon</div>
  <h3>30-Person Network Experiment</h3>
  <p class="dataset-meta">Data Collection: 2023-2024 | N=450 participants</p>
  <p class="dataset-description">This dataset contains complete records from our large-scale network experiments in which participants were arranged in lattice networks of 30 people each. The data includes:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Item adoption decisions with timestamps</li>
    <li>Network configurations</li>
    <li>Pre- and post-experiment surveys</li>
    <li>Narrative understanding assessments</li>
  </ul>
  <div class="dataset-formats">
    <span class="format-badge">CSV</span>
    <span class="format-badge">SQL Dump</span>
    <span class="format-badge">JSON</span>
  </div>
</div>

### Individual Difference Measures

<div class="dataset-card">
  <div class="dataset-status">Coming Soon</div>
  <h3>Individual Adoption Strategies Dataset</h3>
  <p class="dataset-meta">Data Collection: 2023-2024 | N=450 participants</p>
  <p class="dataset-description">This dataset focuses on individual differences in narrative adoption strategies, including:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Personality measures</li>
    <li>Cognitive style assessments</li>
    <li>Information processing tendencies</li>
    <li>Adoption decision patterns</li>
  </ul>
  <div class="dataset-formats">
    <span class="format-badge">CSV</span>
    <span class="format-badge">R Data</span>
  </div>
</div>
