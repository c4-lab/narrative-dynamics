## Papers in Development

<div class="wip-project">
  <h3>
    <span class="status-indicator review">●</span> 
    Inter-group Dynamics in Narrative Adoption
  </h3>
  <p class="project-meta">Lead: Dr. Josh Introne | Expected Completion: Q3 2025</p>
  <p class="project-description">
    This paper examines how group identity influences narrative adoption patterns. Using data from experiments that involve participants from distinct social groups, we investigate whether in-group/out-group dynamics affect the likelihood of incorporating narrative elements from different sources.
  </p>
  <div class="project-progress">
    <div class="progress-bar">
      <div class="progress-fill" style="width: 85%"></div>
    </div>
    <p class="progress-label">Under review at <em>Journal of Communication</em></p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="wip-project">
  <h3>
    <span class="status-indicator active">●</span> 
    Temporal Patterns in Multi-group Narrative Convergence
  </h3>
  <p class="project-meta">Lead: TBD | Expected Completion: Q4 2025</p>
  <p class="project-description">
    This study explores how multiple groups converge (or fail to converge) on shared narratives over time. We're developing new mathematical models that incorporate both within-group and between-group dynamics in information sharing and narrative construction.
  </p>
  <div class="project-progress">
    <div class="progress-bar">
      <div class="progress-fill" style="width: 50%"></div>
    </div>
    <p class="progress-label">Data collection and preliminary analysis complete</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="wip-project">
  <h3>
    <span class="status-indicator early">●</span> 
    Network Topology and Narrative Resilience
  </h3>
  <p class="project-meta">Lead: TBD | Expected Completion: Q2 2026</p>
  <p class="project-description">
    <span class="coming-soon-label">Coming Soon</span> This project will investigate how different network structures affect the resilience of narratives to competing information. We'll be using agent-based simulations and experimental data to identify network properties that promote stable shared understanding versus those that lead to narrative fragmentation.
  </p>
  <div class="project-progress">
    <div class="progress-bar">
      <div class="progress-fill" style="width: 20%"></div>
    </div>
    <p class="progress-label">Research design phase</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Research Data Collection

<div class="wip-project">
  <h3>
    <span class="status-indicator active">●</span> 
    Longitudinal Study of Narrative Evolution
  </h3>
  <p class="project-meta">Data Collection Period: 2024-2026</p>
  <p class="project-description">
    We're currently collecting data for a long-term study on how narratives evolve over extended periods. Unlike our previous experiments that focused on short-term adoption decisions, this study tracks narrative development over months, allowing us to observe more complex evolutionary patterns.
  </p>
  <div class="project-progress">
    <div class="progress-bar">
      <div class="progress-fill" style="width: 40%"></div>
    </div>
    <p class="progress-label">Data collection ongoing (16 of 36 months complete)</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="wip-project">
  <h3>
    <span class="status-indicator concept">●</span> 
    Cross-cultural Narrative Structures
  </h3>
  <p class="project-meta">Planning Stage | Expected Launch: Q3 2025</p>

    <span class="coming-soon-label">Coming Soon</span> We're designing a cross-cultural study to examine whether narrative adoption patterns vary across different cultural contexts. This project will expand our experimental approach to include participants from multiple countries and cultural backgrounds.

## Simulation and Modeling Work

<div class="wip-project">
  <h3>
    <span class="status-indicator advanced">●</span> 
    Enhanced Agent-Based Model for Narrative Diffusion
  </h3>
  <p class="project-meta">Lead: TBD | Expected Release: Q2 2025</p>
  <p class="project-description">
    We're developing an advanced version of our agent-based model that incorporates more sophisticated cognitive mechanisms for narrative processing. This model integrates insights from our experimental findings to create more realistic simulations of narrative diffusion processes.
  </p>
  <div class="project-progress">
    <div class="progress-bar">
      <div class="progress-fill" style="width: 70%"></div>
    </div>
    <p class="progress-label">Core model implementation complete, validation in progress</p>
  </div>
  <p class="project-updates"><strong>Latest Update:</strong> Initial validation results show promising alignment with experimental data.</p>
